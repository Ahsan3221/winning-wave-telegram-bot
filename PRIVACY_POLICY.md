# Privacy Policy — Winning Wave Telegram Bot

**Effective date:** 8 September 2026  
**Last updated:** 8 September 2026

Winning Wave operates the Telegram support bot **@winningwavesupportbot** (the “Bot”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot processes and why.

## 1. Contact

For privacy questions, requests to access, correct or delete information, or concerns about this policy, message **@winningwavesupportbot** on Telegram and use the support chat.

For official announcements and updates, visit **@winningwaveofficial**.

## 2. Information we process

When you use @winningwavesupportbot, we may process:

- your Telegram user ID, display name and username;
- messages, media and files that you choose to send to the Bot;
- your selected game and bonus option, if you choose one;
- source/deep-link information, such as Website or Facebook, when you start the Bot from such a link;
- support-topic ID, timestamps and delivery/status information needed to operate customer support; and
- information you voluntarily provide while speaking with support.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, account credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information through the Bot.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- create and manage your customer-support conversation;
- identify the correct conversation to authorized Winning Wave support staff and respond to you;
- provide game, bonus and general support you request;
- maintain service security, prevent spam or misuse, and fix technical problems;
- understand high-level usage and campaign sources; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce relevant terms where necessary.

## 4. Who can receive information

Your support messages and relevant Telegram details are shared with authorized Winning Wave support staff in a private Telegram support group so they can assist you.

The Bot uses Telegram to provide messaging and may use hosting and database providers to operate the service. Those providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

Winning Wave does not sell personal information.

## 5. Data retention

We keep Bot database records and support information only for as long as reasonably needed for customer support, service security, dispute handling, legal compliance and business records. Our usual retention period is up to **12 months after the last support interaction**, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Telegram and people participating in Telegram conversations may retain chat history according to their own settings, policies and legal obligations.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information and restrict the private support group to authorized staff. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. Please keep your Telegram account secure and do not share unnecessary sensitive information in chat.

## 7. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of your information, or to withdraw consent where applicable.

To make a request, contact **@winningwavesupportbot** through Telegram support. Include your Telegram username or numeric Telegram ID where possible so we can locate the correct record. We may need to verify that the request comes from the relevant account. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or record-keeping purposes.

## 8. International processing

Telegram, hosting providers, authorized staff or technical infrastructure may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

## 9. Children

The Bot is not intended for anyone below the minimum age required to use Telegram or the age required for the services offered in their location. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact @winningwavesupportbot so we can review the matter.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service, practices or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at this location with an updated “Last updated” date.
